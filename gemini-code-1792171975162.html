What you get, and who it is for

A person works through your agreed workflows by hand and sends a prioritized report: what failed, how to reproduce it, and what to fix first. It suits founders, solo developers, small businesses and creators about to launch a site or an AI-built web app.

The five-workflow limit

A workflow is one start-to-finish task a visitor performs, such as creating an account, buying a product or sending a contact form. Before testing begins we agree on up to five. Anything beyond those five is outside the $39 package. Keeping the scope small keeps the price low and the report focused.

How it works

  1. Send the intake request with your URL and up to five workflows.
  2. Scope is confirmed by email, and we agree what will be tested.
  3. Access and payment are arranged. Use a staging site or temporary test account, never a production password. Online checkout is not enabled yet.
  4. Manual testing of the agreed workflows on the agreed browsers and devices.
  5. You receive the report with reproduction steps and priorities.
  6. One retest of the fixes you made to reported issues.

Scope and limitations

  • We report problems we observe. We do not promise to find every defect, and we do not certify that software is bug-free.
  • This is not a security service: no penetration testing, security audit or compliance certification.
  • We do not fix your code. The report tells you what to fix.
  • The operator tests manually. AI may help draft and organize the report, but every reported defect is verified by a person.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need my password?
No. Never send a production password. If a workflow needs a login, create a staging or temporary least-privilege account and share it only after scope is confirmed.
What counts as a workflow?
One complete task, for example "guest checkout" or "sign up and verify email". The package covers up to five.
Is this a security test?
No. It is functional testing: does the site work when people use it.
How fast is delivery?
The target is within 48 hours after scope, access and payment are confirmed. It depends on access, complexity and the agreed scope.
Can I pay online right now?
Not yet. Checkout is not configured, so this site does not take payments. After you send an intake request, payment is arranged directly once scope is confirmed.
